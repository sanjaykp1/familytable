# Catalogue nutrition notes

Updated: 7 August 2026

The catalogue expansion is designed for variety and practical weekday cooking, not as medical or
individual dietary advice. The recipes intentionally rotate fish, lean poultry, tofu, lentils and
beans; include vegetables in the main dish; and use wholegrain grains where they fit naturally.

This follows broad healthy-eating guidance to include a variety of vegetables and fruit, higher-fibre
starchy foods, beans/pulses and protein foods including fish, while favouring minimally processed
foods and unsaturated oils. Sources: [NHS balanced diet guidance](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/eat-well/how-to-eat-a-balanced-diet/eating-a-balanced-diet/),
[NHS 5 A Day](https://www.nhs.uk/healthier-families/food-facts/5-a-day/), and the
[American Heart Association's healthy eating guidance](https://www.heart.org/en/healthy-living/healthy-lifestyle/lifes-essential-8/how-to-eat-better-fact-sheet).

The recipes are editable suggestions. Adapt ingredients and portions for allergies, cultural needs,
medical dietary advice, and what the household will actually enjoy.
